Plums: 0.4 BU per fruit, pits and fresh vs prunes

Fresh plums contain 11 g of carbs per 100 g of flesh — matching apples (1 BU = 109.1 g). One small 40 g plum carries 4.4 g of carbs, or about 0.4 BU, so 2 to 3 medium pitted plums (~110 g) add up to 1 BU.

With a low GI of 39 and naturally small piece size, plums are easy to portion cleanly when you only want 0.4 or 0.8 BU. Since the pit is inedible, weigh the pitted halves or allow a few grams per stone.

Do not confuse fresh plums with dried prunes: prunes jump to ~58 g of carbs per 100 g, putting 1 BU in just 20–21 g (2–3 prunes). Fresh plums work well alongside cottage cheese, plain yogurt or savory roast poultry.

Questions about this food

How many fresh plums make 1 bread unit?

Roughly 2.5 to 3 small plums (40 g flesh each, ~0.4 BU per plum) total around 109 g, or 1 BU at the 12 g norm.

How does fresh plum compare with dried prunes?

Fresh plums have 11 g carbs per 100 g (1 BU = 109 g), while dried prunes concentrate to ~58 g carbs per 100 g (1 BU ≈ 21 g).

How many BU are in 100 g of plum?

100 g of plum contains 11 g of carbs. That is 0.9 BU at the 12 g norm or 1.1 BU at the 10 g norm.

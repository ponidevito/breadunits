How pelmeni (meat dumplings) affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Pelmeni are easy to undercount because a single dumpling weighs just 13 g. The food contains 22 g carbs per 100 g, so 54.5 g of pelmeni equals 1 bread unit at the 12 g norm; some doctors use 10 g. One piece is about 0.03 BU, not zero, and that small fraction matters when the plate fills up. Its GI is 55, the low boundary, with medium starting at 56. Pelmeni may affect blood glucose mainly through the dough. This is reference only; individual response varies.

Meat filling changes the meal, yet the listed carbs describe the finished dumpling, filling and wrapper together. Because each piece is so light, weighing the serving or logging the whole portion is more useful than rounding every dumpling away. Recipes vary, so use the table value for a general estimate rather than trying to invent a special number for one family recipe.

What arrives alongside pelmeni often decides the total. Sour cream, broth and a side salad each deserve their own line in a log, and boiled potato or sweet tea should not be treated as part of the same bread unit. Keep the sauce bowl visible while you eat. A calculator is a reference aid, not a statement that any particular plate will behave predictably.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of pelmeni (meat dumplings)?

100 g of pelmeni (meat dumplings) contains 22 g of carbs. That is 1.8 BU at the 12 g norm or 2.2 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much pelmeni (meat dumplings) makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 54.5 g of pelmeni (meat dumplings). At 10 g it is 45.5 g.

What is the glycemic index of pelmeni (meat dumplings)?

The GI listed for this food is 55 (low). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

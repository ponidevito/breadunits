Pelmeni: counting by the dozen (10 dumplings ≈ 2.4 BU) and wrapper thickness

Meat-filled pelmeni average 22 g of carbs per 100 g, putting 1 BU at 54.5 g. One small 13 g dumpling holds roughly 0.2 BU (0.24 BU exact), which adds up quickly across a bowl: 10 dumplings (~130 g) equal about 2.4 BU, and a hearty 15-piece serving (~200 g) reaches roughly 3.6 BU.

Their GI of 55 sits at the upper edge of the low band because meat protein and fat temper the wheat wrapper. Wrapper-to-filling ratio varies widely across brands, though — thick-dough frozen dumplings can hit 28–32 g carbs per 100 g, so check the package label.

Sour cream, broth, vinegar or a pat of butter add negligible carbs, whereas ketchup, sweet chili sauce or a slice of bread on the side should be logged separately.

Questions about this food

How many bread units are in 10 or 15 pelmeni?

At 13 g per dumpling, 10 pelmeni (~130 g) equal roughly 2.4 BU and 15 pelmeni (~195 g) equal about 3.6 BU. For larger homemade dumplings, weighing the bowl is best.

Why do different brands of pelmeni have different carb counts?

Dough thickness and filling ratio vary considerably — commercial packages can range anywhere from 20 g to 32 g of carbs per 100 g.

How many BU are in 100 g of pelmeni?

100 g of pelmeni contains 22 g of carbs. That is 1.8 BU at the 12 g norm or 2.2 BU at the 10 g norm.

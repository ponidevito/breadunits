How a banana affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Banana brings 21 g carbs per 100 g, so the calculator places one bread unit at about 57.1 g of peeled fruit when using the 12 g norm. Some doctors calculate bread units from 10 g instead; this page keeps 12 g so the numbers stay consistent. A typical piece here weighs 120 g and equals about 0.3 BU.

With GI 51, banana sits in the low band, defined as 55 or below. Medium falls between 56 and 69, while high starts at 70. That band is reference only: banana may raise blood glucose, and individual response varies with ripeness, the rest of the meal, and personal tolerance.

Riper bananas usually taste sweeter and feel softer, while firmer fruit tastes less sweet; the table still uses the same 21 g per 100 g figure. Choose whole banana when you want the simplest match to the calculator, and pair it with plain yogurt or nuts. For recipes, weigh the peeled fruit instead of relying on the look of a bunch.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of banana?

100 g of banana contains 21 g of carbs. That is 1.8 BU at the 12 g norm or 2.1 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much banana makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 57.1 g of banana. At 10 g it is 47.6 g.

What is the glycemic index of banana?

The GI listed for this food is 51 (low). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

Plain yogurt: 200 ml per BU and the flavored-cup sugar trap

Unsweetened plain yogurt averages 6 g of carbs per 100 ml, putting 1 BU at 200 ml (roughly one standard cup). Strained Greek yogurt often goes even lower — around 3.5–4.5 g carbs per 100 g — while delivering nearly twice the protein.

With a low GI of 35, plain yogurt is an ideal base for breakfasts and savory dips: its protein and acidity help buffer the carbs from berries, sliced fruit or oats added to the bowl.

The supermarket trap is flavored, fruit-on-the-bottom and low-fat dessert yogurts, which frequently jump to 12–16 g carbs per 100 g from added sugar and starch. Buying plain white yogurt and topping it yourself with fresh berries or nuts keeps both carbs and ingredients transparent.

Questions about this food

How does plain Greek yogurt compare with regular plain yogurt?

Regular plain yogurt has ~5–6 g carbs per 100 ml, while strained Greek yogurt typically drops to 3.5–4.5 g per 100 g with higher protein. Check your brand's label.

How many bread units are in a 125 g pot of plain yogurt?

A 125 ml pot contains around 7.5 g of carbs — roughly 0.6 BU at the 12 g norm.

How many BU are in 100 ml of plain yogurt?

100 ml of plain yogurt contains 6 g of carbs. That is 0.5 BU at the 12 g norm or 0.6 BU at the 10 g norm.

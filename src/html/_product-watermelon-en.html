How watermelon affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Watermelon is a useful example of why carbs per 100 g and GI answer different questions. It has 8 g carbs per 100 g, so 150 g of watermelon equals one bread unit under the 12 g norm. Some doctors calculate bread units from 10 g; this tool does not. There is no fixed piece weight here, because slices and wedges vary.

With GI 72, watermelon is in the high band, which starts at 70. Low is 55 or below and medium covers 56 to 69. That high label is reference only: watermelon may affect blood glucose differently from low-GI fruit, and individual response varies with the amount eaten and the rest of the meal.

Since there is no standard piece in this entry, weighing the edible portion is the clearest approach. Choose fresh watermelon rather than sweetened drinks made from it if you want the 8 g per 100 g figure to apply. Eating it as part of a meal with protein and fat can be more useful than treating it as an isolated snack; avoid adding syrup or sweet sauces.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of watermelon?

100 g of watermelon contains 8 g of carbs. That is 0.7 BU at the 12 g norm or 0.8 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much watermelon makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 150 g of watermelon. At 10 g it is 125 g.

What is the glycemic index of watermelon?

The GI listed for this food is 72 (high). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

How rye bread affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Rye bread is often chosen for its heartier character, but the arithmetic still matters: 42 g of carbs per 100 g. At the standard 12 g per bread unit, 28.6 g of rye bread equals 1 BU. A 10 g convention exists as well, so make sure you and your clinician are talking about the same norm. All of these figures are reference only.

Its GI of 58 places rye bread in the medium band. Compared with high-GI options, glucose may shift less abruptly, although that is not a promise about any particular person. Individual response varies with the toppings, the size of the meal and your wider routine. Treat the GI as a way of comparing foods, not as a detailed forecast of your own readings.

Because rye loaves are frequently dense, a slice that looks modest may weigh more than expected. Weigh one slice and compare it with the 28.6 g benchmark before you add up bread units for the day. Vegetables, lean meat or cheese make a natural partner and can help you feel satisfied with fewer slices. The calculator is a planning aid; it does not replace personalised advice.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of rye bread?

100 g of rye bread contains 42 g of carbs. That is 3.5 BU at the 12 g norm or 4.2 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much rye bread makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 28.6 g of rye bread. At 10 g it is 23.8 g.

What is the glycemic index of rye bread?

The GI listed for this food is 58 (medium). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

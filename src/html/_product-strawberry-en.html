How strawberry affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Strawberries have a low carb figure per 100 g, which means the weight for one bread unit is comparatively large. The entry uses 7 g carbs per 100 g; at the 12 g norm, 171.4 g of strawberries makes 1 BU. The alternative 10 g rule for bread units would produce another result, so this page sticks to one standard. No typical piece weight is assigned.

GI 40 is in the low band, defined as 55 or below, while medium covers 56 to 69 and high starts at 70. Strawberries may still affect blood glucose because they do contain carbs. GI is reference only, and individual response varies with ripeness, the rest of the meal, and the person eating them.

Remove the green tops before weighing if you are following the edible-part basis used here. Ripe strawberries smell fragrant and look soft, but the calculator still uses 7 g per 100 g rather than a ripeness adjustment. Fresh berries fit this entry; sweetened purees and jam do not. Plain yogurt or unsweetened cheese is a natural companion that keeps the fruit central.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of strawberry?

100 g of strawberry contains 7 g of carbs. That is 0.6 BU at the 12 g norm or 0.7 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much strawberry makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 171.4 g of strawberry. At 10 g it is 142.9 g.

What is the glycemic index of strawberry?

The GI listed for this food is 40 (low). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

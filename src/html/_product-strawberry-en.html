Strawberries: a generous 171 g per BU and keeping toppings simple

Strawberries are one of the most generous fruits for carb counting at just 7 g of carbs per 100 g. One bread unit buys you a full 171.4 g bowl — roughly 10 to 14 medium berries.

With a low GI of 40, high water content and fiber-rich seeds, fresh strawberries deliver a gentle glucose profile that fits comfortably into almost any meal plan.

Keep preparations simple: fresh or unsweetened frozen berries match the 7 g benchmark, whereas macerating them in sugar, dipping them in chocolate or choosing strawberry jam changes the count completely. Pair them with plain Greek yogurt, whipped unsweetened cream or cottage cheese.

Questions about this food

About how many strawberries equal 1 bread unit?

One BU is 171.4 g of hulled strawberries — roughly 10 to 14 medium berries, or one heaped cup.

Do frozen strawberries have the same bread units as fresh ones?

Plain unsweetened frozen strawberries have the same 7 g carbs per 100 g; just check the bag to confirm no syrup or sugar was added.

How many BU are in 100 g of strawberries?

100 g of strawberries contains 7 g of carbs. That is 0.6 BU at the 12 g norm or 0.7 BU at the 10 g norm.

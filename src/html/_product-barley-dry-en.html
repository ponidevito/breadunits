How dry pearl barley affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Pearl barley belongs to the group of grains that are counted dry, before the pot changes their weight. Dry pearl barley has 67 g of carbs per 100 g. Under the standard of 12 g of carbs per bread unit, 17.9 g of dry groats equals 1 BU. A 10 g convention is also in use among some specialists. All these numbers are reference values and need individual interpretation.

The GI of pearl barley is 28, firmly in the low band. Glucose may change quite slowly after eating it, but that observation should not be turned into a medical claim. Individual response varies with how well the barley is cooked, what accompanies it and what the rest of your day looks like. The GI simply lets you compare this grain with others; it cannot forecast your personal result.

Barley makes a thick, substantial porridge, which is exactly why the dry weigh-in matters: 17.9 g is one bread unit before cooking begins. The finished portion looks much larger, so make your note at the start rather than guessing afterwards. Braised vegetables, meat or mushrooms are natural partners and make the meal varied without putting all the attention on the grain. Keep the reference nature of the figures in mind.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of dry pearl barley?

100 g of dry pearl barley contains 67 g of carbs. That is 5.6 BU at the 12 g norm or 6.7 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much dry pearl barley makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 17.9 g of dry pearl barley. At 10 g it is 14.9 g.

What is the glycemic index of dry pearl barley?

The GI listed for this food is 28 (low). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.

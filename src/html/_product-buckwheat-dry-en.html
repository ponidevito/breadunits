How dry buckwheat affects blood sugar: portions and pairings

Buckwheat is one of those foods where the state of the grain decides the count: you weigh it dry, before cooking. Dry buckwheat contains 62 g of carbs per 100 g. With 1 bread unit set at 12 g of carbs, 19.4 g of the dry groats makes 1 BU. A 10 g norm is also used by some doctors, so confirm the convention behind any figure you compare. These are reference values only.

Buckwheat has a GI of 50, which belongs to the low band. Glucose may change more gradually than after higher-GI foods, but individual response varies from person to person. How the groats are cooked, what shares the plate and what you eat during the day all matter. The GI helps you compare grains; it is not a promise of a particular effect or a medical recommendation.

The simplest habit is to weigh the groats before they ever touch water, because cooking changes the weight considerably. Use the 19.4 g figure for one bread unit and keep your record at the dry stage, then simply cook what you measured. Buckwheat works well with vegetables, mushrooms or a protein dish, so a varied meal is easy to build without doing arithmetic over a finished plate of porridge.

Questions about this food

How many BU are in 100 g of dry buckwheat?

100 g of dry buckwheat contains 62 g of carbs. That is 5.2 BU at the 12 g norm or 6.2 BU at the 10 g norm.

How much dry buckwheat makes 1 BU?

At the 12 g carb norm, 1 BU corresponds to 19.4 g of dry buckwheat. At 10 g it is 16.1 g.

What is the glycemic index of dry buckwheat?

The GI listed for this food is 50 (low). It is an approximate reference value, not an individual recommendation.
